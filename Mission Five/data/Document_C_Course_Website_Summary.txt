The course's fictional mission concerns corrupted ship archives and the restoration of source-grounded answers. Its Week 5 lore describes an archive crisis involving the Kepler Concordance and a NotebookLM-style protocol for verifiable information.

The protocol calls for claims tied to inspectable source passages, acknowledgement when the available sources cannot answer a question, and conversational audio that remains faithful to the supplied material. The mission's audio debrief illustrates those principles in a fictional starship setting; it is not empirical evidence that a real retrieval system achieves perfect citation accuracy.

The assignment asks for hybrid retrieval, a NotebookLM citation and refusal audit, and a dual-host Audio Overview evaluation. The archive lore provides motivation and expected behavior, not hardware temperatures, propulsion serial codes, or measured safety guarantees.
